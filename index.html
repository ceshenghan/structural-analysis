Help / conventions

Units (numeric mode). kN, m, kN/m, kN·m; EI in kN·m², EA in kN (defaults EI = 10⁵ kN·m², EA = 2×10⁶ kN). Tick “neglect axial deformation” to make all members axially rigid (EA = ∞) while keeping numeric EI.

Symbolic mode. Members carry a stiffness factor × EI (1, 2, 1/2 …) and are axially rigid, as in slope‑deflection / moment distribution. Load values may contain one symbol each (P, q, M0, …). The structure is analysed once for every symbol (unit value) and all results are exact linear combinations, e.g. 3/8 qL − 1/4 P. Forces and moments are independent of EI; rotations and deflections are reported as …/EI. With “lengths in multiples of L” the coordinates are read in units of L and the power of L in every result follows from dimensional analysis (force ∝ qL, moment ∝ qL², rotation ∝ qL³/EI, deflection ∝ qL⁴/EI) – use each symbol for one load type only (P for forces, q for distributed loads, M0 for couples). The “plot values” of the symbols are used only to draw the diagrams and the deformed shape and to locate extrema. If axial rigidity makes an axial force statically indeterminate (e.g. a beam between two pins) that force is reported as 0 with a warning.

Member direction. Members are created from their left end to their right end (vertical members from bottom to top); this defines the local x axis, and the local y axis is x rotated 90° counter‑clockwise (up for a beam, to the left for a column). The direction can be reversed in the member editor. N: tension positive. V: positive when the resultant transverse force on the part between the start and the section acts towards +y (for a beam: upward force on the left part); drawn on the +y side. M: positive when the −y side is in tension (bottom tension for a beam); the moment diagram is always drawn on the tension side.

Tools. Add node on a member splits it (loads are split consistently). Add member: click two nodes (clicking empty space creates a node). Release: click a node (all member ends there), near a member end (that end only) or in the middle of a member (a node with the release is inserted); the same release again removes it. Support / Node load: click a node (or a member, which is split). Member load: click a member. Move: drag a node. Delete on an intermediate node of a straight member merges the two segments. Select opens an editor for a node (coordinates, support, load) or a member (stiffness, releases, loads, direction). Drag empty space to pan, wheel to zoom, right‑click / Esc to cancel, Ctrl+Z to undo.

Analysis. Direct stiffness method, 3 DOF per node, Euler–Bernoulli members with exact (work‑equivalent) member loads. Released member‑end DOFs are condensed out and recovered afterwards (kinks / slips appear in the deformed shape). Supports and axial‑rigidity conditions are treated as constraints (null‑space method); their multipliers are the reactions / axial forces. Internal forces are evaluated by statics along each member and deflections are the exact Euler–Bernoulli solution, so textbook fractions (5/384, 1/8, 3/8 …) are reproduced to machine precision. Degree of static indeterminacy: ns = 3m + r − 3j − ec (m members, r reaction components, j connected nodes, ec equations of condition; at a node where every member end is hinged ec counts one less). A mechanism (singular stiffness on the constrained space) is reported as unstable.
